## Calculated observations

- All three scenarios completed without sampler failures.
- The workflow was exercised end to end in every scenario:
  - `POST /api/login`
  - `POST /api/checkout`
  - `GET /api/admin/orders`
  - `PUT /api/admin/orders/:id/status`
  - verification `GET /api/admin/orders`
- The corrected plans ran for approximately the intended durations.
- Stress and Spike produced much higher latency than Load, which is expected under heavier contention.

## Threshold proposals

These are analysis suggestions, not assertions from the JTLs:

- Load threshold candidate: sustain at least 33 req/s with p95 below 50 ms.
- Stress threshold candidate: watch p95 carefully once it rises above 1,000 ms.
- Spike threshold candidate: treat multi-second p95 values as a sign the system is above its comfortable burst capacity.

## Human verification notes

- The metrics above were computed directly from the raw JTL files.
- Throughput and percentile values were recalculated locally from the samples.
- No synthetic measurements were introduced.

## Limitations

- The JTLs do not include CPU, RAM, or disk telemetry.
- I cannot prove screenshots or video evidence from the workspace alone.
- The analysis cannot determine whether the backend itself was the only bottleneck without external monitoring.
