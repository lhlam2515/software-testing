# Task Scenarios

## Primary scenario

As a student, search for a suitable event, open the event details, register for the event, and verify that the registration appears in your account or confirmation area.

### Goal

Complete the event registration flow without help and confirm the final status.

### Screens/features covered

- Event search or browse view
- Event details and registration action
- Registration confirmation or status page

### Notes

- This is a goal-oriented task, not a click-by-click script.
- Do not coach participants through the interface.
- Encourage think-aloud behavior throughout the task.

## Alternate scenario A

As a student, update or cancel a registration after noticing that the wrong event was selected.

### Why this scenario exists

- Tests recovery and trust in the system state.
- Covers confirmation and error-prevention behavior.

## Alternate scenario B

As a staff or admin user, review a participant registration and verify that the displayed status matches the expected enrollment state.

### Why this scenario exists

- Adds coverage for a secondary role.
- Exercises an additional status/check screen if the primary scenario is completed too quickly.
