# AI Critique Template

## Overview

This placeholder critique is intended to be completed after the real testing work is performed. It should contain 200–300 words and should critique the AI assistance used during the assignment rather than simply describing the workflow.

## Draft Structure

- Briefly describe where the AI assistance was useful.
- Explain where the AI output was incomplete, generic, or misleading.
- Note whether the AI missed accessibility, localization, or interaction issues that required human reasoning.
- State what principle you learned about collaborating with AI in a testing assignment.

## Placeholder Text

The AI tool was helpful for generating a first version of the GUI checklist and for structuring the report templates, especially when the assignment required a large number of deliverables. However, its output was often too generic and did not fully capture the specific context of the EMS interface, especially for accessibility, localization, and interaction details. Some items were plausible but not specific enough to be executed reliably, and the AI often suggested broad design recommendations without identifying the exact screen state or evidence needed for a real test. This limitation matters in GUI usability testing because a checklist item is only useful when it can be observed, explained, and verified on an actual screen. The main lesson learned is that AI should serve as a starting point or accelerator for analysis, not as a substitute for human judgment. In this assignment, the most valuable role of AI was to accelerate preparation, but the final correctness still depended on human review, real execution, and careful interpretation of observed evidence.
